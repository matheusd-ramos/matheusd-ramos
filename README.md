## About

I am a student at UFCI-MG (formerly CEFET-MG). I have experience in software development, agile methodologies, and project management through COMPET, where I served as a Scrum Master and coordinated teams to deliver planned tasks.

I also work on Project Salamanca, in partnership with Aeropalynology researchers at the University of Salamanca. I integrate a Latin-language speech recognition microservice with the Pollen Catcher web platform and handle ongoing updates to the site. I work mainly with C, C++, Java, Python, TypeScript/JavaScript, Bash, and SQL.

- 🎯 Open to internships in backend, data/AI, product, or agile teams, and still figuring out where I fit best
- 🌎 International project experience with the University of Salamanca
- 📍 Belo Horizonte, Brazil
